About

I am a Ph.D. student in Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Dr. Chang Liu. I am also completing clinical audiology training toward professional certification. My long-term goal is to connect hearing science research with clinically meaningful approaches to communication and hearing rehabilitation.

Research

My research focuses primarily on speech perception in noise and other challenging listening environments.

Speech Perception in Noise

Examining how listeners understand speech across different types of background noise and listening conditions.

Cognitive and Social Factors

Studying how working memory, vocabulary knowledge, prior experience, and social contact affect speech understanding.

Auditory Electrophysiology

Using electrophysiological methods to investigate auditory learning, emotional processing, and neural responses to sound.
