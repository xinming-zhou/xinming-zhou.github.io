I am a Ph.D. student in Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Dr. Chang Liu. I study how cognitive, neural, and social factors contribute to individual differences in challenging speech perception using behavioral and electrophysiological methods. I am also completing clinical audiology training toward professional certification.

Clinical Training

I have completed more than 500 hours of supervised clinical audiology training across university clinics, private practices, otolaryngology settings, and community screening programs.

My experience includes hearing evaluation, hearing aid consultation and follow-up, tinnitus assessment, cochlear implant services, vestibular testing, auditory processing assessment, pediatric and adult screening, and auditory electrophysiology.
